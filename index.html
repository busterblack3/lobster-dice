How we play

  • On your turn, roll the two dice as often as you like. Safe rolls add to your turn pile.
  • Roll one 🦞 (a 1): your turn ends and you lose the turn pile. Banked points survive.
  • Roll two 🦞🦞: turn ends and your whole score goes to 0.
  • Doubles (not both 🦞): pips count but you must roll again — banking locked.
  • Getting in: score stays 0 until you bank 21+ on a single turn. Then it counts.
  • Win: first to reach or pass the 100 by banking wins.
